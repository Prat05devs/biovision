Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about BioVision. Still stuck? Email support@biovision.live and we usually reply within two working days.

How do I get the iPhone app?

BioVision is currently in beta on TestFlight. Ask the BioVision team for an invitation, install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the invitation link on your iPhone.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 16.4 or later with a front camera. The scan works best on iPhone 11 or newer.

The scan says "We couldn't read a clear pulse"

Face a window or bright, even light, rest your phone on a table at eye level, remove glasses if possible, and keep your head still for the full 30 seconds.

The eye photo was rejected

Gently pull down your lower eyelid so the inner pink area is clearly visible, avoid flash and glare, and hold the phone steady until the photo is sharp.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The camera video, photos and your answers are analysed on your iPhone and are not uploaded or stored. See the Privacy Policy.

How accurate are the results?

Results are estimates and can be affected by light, movement, make-up and camera quality. Haemoglobin is estimated from eyelid colour, not a blood test. Always confirm important results with a doctor or laboratory test.

Can I change the language?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and choose English or Hindi.

This is an emergency

Do not use the app. Call 112 now. For mental-health crisis support, call Tele-MANAS on 14416.
