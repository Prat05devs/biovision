Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

Summary

BioVision analyses your scan on your iPhone. We do not have accounts, servers that receive your health data, advertising or tracking.

Camera

The front camera is used only during a scan. Video frames are analysed in memory to measure your pulse and are never recorded or uploaded. One face photo and two eye photos are taken to analyse skin signs and estimate haemoglobin. They stay in the app's temporary storage and are removed by the system.

Your answers and profile

Age, sex, pregnancy, height, weight and your answers are used only to calculate your report. They stay in memory and are cleared when you start a new scan or close the app.

Location

If you choose to find nearby healthcare, your location is used on your iPhone to sort the list. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Stored on your iPhone

Only your language choice and that you accepted these terms are saved on your device.

Sharing

Your report leaves the app only when you tap Share and choose where to send it. Photos are never included.

Third parties

BioVision contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and does not sell or share personal data.

Children

BioVision is designed for adults. Children under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will ask you to review it again before you continue.

Contact

For questions, use the support link on BioVision's App Store page.
