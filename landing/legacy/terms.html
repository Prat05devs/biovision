About BioVision

BioVision is a health-information prototype. It estimates health indicators from your camera and your answers and suggests which kind of doctor may help.

Not medical advice

BioVision is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. Its estimates can be wrong. Always confirm results with a qualified doctor or laboratory test, and never delay or ignore medical advice because of the app.

Emergencies

If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, severe bleeding, or thoughts of harming yourself, call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416 immediately.

Accuracy

Lighting, movement, make-up, skin tone and camera quality affect the results. Haemoglobin is an estimate from eyelid colour, not a blood test. Heart-rate readings are not suitable for diagnosing heart conditions.

Doctor directory

Doctor and hospital details come from the hospitals' public websites and may change. Confirm timings and availability with the hospital.

Your responsibility

Use BioVision only for yourself or with the consent of the person being scanned, and follow the on-screen instructions for eye photos.
