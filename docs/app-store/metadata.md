## Description
BioVision turns your iPhone camera into a quick, private health check.

30-SECOND FACE SCAN
• Heart rate and heart-rate variability (RMSSD, SDNN)
• Stress index and recovery score
• Breathing rate
• Face and skin signs: under-eye shading, eye redness, lip colour, skin tone
• Live pulse waveform while you scan

EYE CHECK
• Estimated haemoglobin from the colour of your inner lower eyelid, compared with WHO ranges for your age and sex

SMART HEALTH QUESTIONS
• Tell us what is bothering you — tiredness, breathing, heart, skin, mood, headaches, sugar and thyroid, stomach, joints, ENT, eyes or women's health
• Danger signs send you straight to emergency help

A CLEAR REPORT
• Every result explained in plain language with its normal range
• The right doctor to see, how soon, and which tests to ask about
• Share a PDF report with your doctor or family

FIND CARE
• Over 200 doctors and hospitals in Dehradun from hospitals' official websites, filtered by speciality, with directions and call buttons

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything is analysed on your iPhone. No account, no uploads, no ads, no tracking. English and Hindi.

BioVision provides health information and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. Always confirm results with a doctor. In an emergency call 112.

## App Review notes
No login is required.

How to test:
1. Accept the welcome screen and tap "Start health scan".
2. Enter age, sex, height and weight.
3. Allow camera access. In even light, fit your face inside the mesh and tap the round button for the 30-second scan (a physical iPhone is required; the simulator has no camera).
4. Take two eye photos (gently pull down the lower eyelid), answer the questions and view the report.
5. "Share PDF report" and "Find … near you" are on the report screen. Settings (gear icon on the home screen) holds the privacy policy, terms and medical information.

All analysis runs on the device. The app does not transmit health data, has no server component and contains no analytics or advertising. Camera frames are processed in memory and never recorded.

The app provides general health information with clear notices that it is not a diagnosis and does not replace a doctor; emergency answers direct users to 112 and Tele-MANAS 14416. Methods and data sources are described in Settings → Medical information.
